About Us:


When our company was founded, our mission was to provide accessible and reliable service to all dog owners in the West Georgia area. In light of this, we are proud to say that we have made good on that mission for 13 years now. We have spent countless hours caring for and looking after our clients’ best friends, and will continue to spend countless more well into the future. If you are a dog owner looking for grooming, sitting, or training services, then you have come to the right place. We would be happy if you could join us.
